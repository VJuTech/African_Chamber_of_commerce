# Chapter 10: Business Account Registration Specification

## Functional requirements covered

- ACC-FRS-BIZ-001: Initiate Business Registration
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-002: Capture Business Information
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-003: Prevent Duplicate Business Registration
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-004: Assign Business Ownership
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-005: Add Organization Administrators
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-006: Business Status Management
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-007: Submit Business for Verification
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-008: Save Draft Business Registration
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-009: Business Registration Notification
- ACC-FRS-BIZ-010: Business Registration Audit Logging

## Notes

The implementation in this repository includes the functional workflow for creating business accounts, validating required information, assigning ownership, preventing duplicate registrations, saving drafts, submitting for verification, notifying the owner, and recording lifecycle events in audit logs.
